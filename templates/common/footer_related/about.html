{% trans "What is behind the name Mom to be?" %}

{% blocktrans %} The idea behind the name comes from my belief that a mother is not created from a positive test, a long pregnancy, or even the first birth. Becoming a mother is a process that likely continues for a very long time—or never really ends. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "Who is behind this website?" %}

{% blocktrans %} I am Maria, from Bulgaria, but I have been living in Vienna, Austria for 13 years. I graduated from a language high school in Plovdiv with Spanish as my major, but life circumstances brought me to a German-speaking country, where the first challenge was learning this difficult language in order to study at university. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} The system here allows you to enroll in a German course only after you have enrolled at one of the Vienna universities. I did exactly that, choosing a bachelor's degree in Technical Chemistry at the Vienna University of Technology. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} After one year of effort and exams, I successfully passed the language certificate and started my studies. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} After the first year, however, I realized that it was not the right path for me. I changed my major and university and took the entrance exam for Journalism at the University of Vienna. After passing the exam and several very challenging semesters, I realized this was the path I wanted to follow, even though I had to study journalism in German and deal with professors with strong Austrian accents. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} For someone still learning the language, it was normal to fail sometimes, although I did not understand that at the time. Every bad grade discouraged me. It was demotivating and affected me negatively. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} At the same time, I met many colleagues—Bulgarians and others—and my social life was quite dynamic. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} I found what was supposed to be a temporary job, which gradually became permanent, and university took a secondary or even tertiary role. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} A few years and a pandemic later, I find myself in a wonderful place in life: I have a great husband, a small son, and a cozy home here in the heart of the classical capital of Europe. I write this small journal with the hope of being useful to someone like me, or anyone going through similar experiences. {% endblocktrans %}
